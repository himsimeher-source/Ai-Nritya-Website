Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about practicing with Nritya AI

Can I paste any YouTube dance video? ▼
Yes! You can paste any standard YouTube URL (including Shorts and share links) or upload your own video file (.mp4, .webm). The AI will process the performance, isolate the key poses, and segment the routine into numbered steps with counts.
Do I need a special camera or motion capture suit? ▼
Not at all. Nritya AI works directly with standard laptop webcams, tablet cameras, or smartphone lenses. MediaPipe Computer Vision extracts 33 3D skeletal landmarks in real time right in your browser.
How are classical Adavus graded? ▼
For Bharatnatyam and Kathak, the model evaluates anatomical angular standards—such as Araimandi knee flexion (~115°), Natyarambha arm level, and Tatkar sole strike acoustic resonance—comparing your posture against classical dance master keyframes.
Is my practice video saved or uploaded to the cloud? ▼
Never. All camera posture processing is performed client-side on your local device. Your video feed is strictly private and never uploaded or stored.
