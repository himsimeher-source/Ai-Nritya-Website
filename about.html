Our Five Technological Pillars

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Orchestrates intelligent tutoring dialogue, personalizes drill recommendations based on historic drift patterns, and breaks down complex routines into digestible 4-count and 8-count phrases.

🧠 Machine Learning

Spatio-temporal neural networks trained on thousands of verified dance sequences evaluate movement transitions, continuity, and stylistic authenticity.

👁️ Computer Vision

Extracts high-resolution spatial features, performs optical flow velocity tracking, and isolates body contours from dynamic performance backgrounds.

📐 Pose Estimation

MediaPipe Pose delivers sub-millimeter 33-point 3D landmark tracking at 60 FPS directly within standard browser webcams without requiring specialized wearable sensors.

💃 Dance Education

Grounded in authentic Shastric treatises (Natya Shastra & Abhinaya Darpana) alongside modern street dance pedagogy, ensuring cultural integrity is preserved.
